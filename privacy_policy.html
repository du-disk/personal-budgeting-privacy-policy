Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 14, 2026

Welcome to Personal Budgeting. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a secure experience when using our mobile application. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of your personal data.

1. Data Collection & Processing

We collect and process various types of information to provide you with the best possible financial management experience:

  • Account Authentication: We utilize Google Sign-In for secure registration and authentication. This provides us with your name, email address, and profile picture.
  • Financial Data: Any transactional data (income, expenses, and account balances) entered into the Service is stored securely via Firebase Firestore Cloud to facilitate cross-device synchronization and data persistence.
  • Visual Documentation: With your explicit consent, we may access your device's camera and gallery to allow you to upload images of receipts or transaction documents. These assets are stored using Firebase Storage.
Special Note on AI & Audio Data: To facilitate voice-to-text recording, the Service requires access to your device's microphone. We utilize high-performance Artificial Intelligence (AI) to transcribe your speech into text in real-time. We do not record, store, or monitor your raw audio files on our servers. The audio data is processed transiently and only for the duration of the transcription.

2. Purpose of Processing

Your data is processed based on the following legal and operational grounds:

  • To provide core financial tracking functionality and cross-device synchronization.
  • To enable innovative voice-recording features leveraging AI technologies.
  • To enhance application stability and perform diagnostics through crash reporting.
  • To ensure the data integrity and security of your personal financial records.

3. Security Standards

We implement industry-standard security measures, including Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) for local data where applicable and secure SSL/TLS protocols for all data transmitted to our cloud servers. For enhanced security, we support Biometric Authentication (Face ID/Fingerprint). All biometric processing occurs exclusively on the hardware-level of your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

4. Third-Party Integrations

We integrate with trusted third-party services to deliver professional-grade functionality:

  • Google Play Services: Fundamental app infrastructure.
  • Firebase (Google LLC): Cloud storage, authentication, and performance monitoring.
  • Speech Recognition & AI APIs: Real-time secure transcription services.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your data only for as long as you maintain an active account with us. You have the total "Right to be Forgotten." You may delete your account and all associated data directly within the "Settings" menu of the application, which will trigger a permanent deletion across all our cloud databases.
